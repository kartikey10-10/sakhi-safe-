PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Clear about what is connected.

Sakhi Safe is an evolving app. SMS is not sent until you confirm in your messaging app. Calling 112 opens your phone dialer. There is no police dispatch, cloud alert delivery, or connected companion device in the current demo. Community sample posts are not publicly published.
